description: Monthly review of what's working, with campaign ideas
---
Use the `cgw-studio` MCP server. Call `get_guidelines` and `get_metrics` with `days: 90`, plus
`get_schedule` and `get_events`.

Write a short report (no more than a page):
1. What performed best and worst, by pillar, channel and format (photo, carousel, video, text),
   with the actual numbers. Note how few posts some conclusions rest on.
2. Three concrete changes for next month (mix, timing, channels, hooks).
3. Three campaign ideas for the next 4-6 weeks, tied to real upcoming events or the season and the
   two-month audience block in the guidelines.
Don't create posts. End by asking which ideas I want turned into drafts.
