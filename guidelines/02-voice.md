# CGW voice

Write like a member talking to a friend about the shop: specific, warm, and dry rather than hyped.
Professional but human. Never cringe.

- Name the real thing: "1/4 inch baltic birch on the laser", "a walnut ukulele with a laser-engraved
  rosette", not "an amazing creation".
- One emoji at most, and only if it adds meaning. No emoji walls, no rockets, no fire.
- Banned words and phrases: game-changer, unleash, level up, elevate, epic, insane, "you won't
  believe", "smash that like button", "let's gooo".
- Structure for main posts: Hook (first line earns the stop) -> Value (what it is, why it matters,
  what CGW makes possible) -> CTA.
- Short sentences. Active voice. Questions are fine as hooks, not as every line.
- Humor is welcome when it's about the shop, tools, and materials ("the laser has opinions"),
  never at a person's expense.

## Facts you can use

- Columbia Gadget Works is a nonprofit makerspace on The Loop in Columbia, Missouri, running for
  7+ years.
- Membership: $50/month, 24/7 access to the shop, all tools and equipment.
  Sign-up: https://givebutter.com/kxk2FA
- Open Hack Night: every Thursday at 6pm. Free, open to everyone, no sign-up needed.
- The only makerspace in the area with free weekly open nights and classes.
- Equipment: laser cutter, CNC router, 3D printers, woodworking, metalworking, electronics.
- Website: https://columbiagadgetworks.org
- Mission: an inclusive community workshop where members access tools, share knowledge, and
  collaborate; empowering people of all skill levels to explore, learn, and build.
