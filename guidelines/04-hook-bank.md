- Get 24/7 access to laser cutters, CNC routers, 3D printers, and more—without turning your living room into a workshop.
- With the right tools (and a little trial and error), you can repair more than you think and keep good stuff out of the landfill.
- What you're missing isn't motivation—it's access, space, and a place where progress actually happens.
- Members get 24/7 access and the freedom to work independently—no training hoops required.
- Use high-end equipment like CNC routers and vinyl cutters without committing your entire budget to one project.
- With access to real tools and space, you can repair, rebuild, and extend the life of the things you care about.
- When you hit that 'why isn't this working' moment, having tools and a community to experiment with makes all the difference.
- This is a space built for figuring things out, making mistakes, and getting better as you go.
- With 24/7 access, you can act on your ideas instead of writing them down and forgetting them later.
- Make repair easier, faster, and way more doable with the right tools all in one place.
- Columbia Gadget Works is a nonprofit—we're here for the community, not the profit margin.
- For $50/month, you get a full machine shop. No lease, no overhead, no buying tools you'll use once.
- Our community includes beginners and experts—and both are equally welcome from day one.
- Thursday open hack nights are free—but membership means you can come back any time, day or night.
